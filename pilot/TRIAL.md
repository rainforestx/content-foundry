## Design

Two cycles in which the session runs the full pipeline with no per-step
operator trigger: subject selection from the documented demand evidence,
grounding, production, adversarial review, fix loop, integration, and
render delivery. The operator's role narrows to exactly one act per
cycle: acceptance on the rendered artifact. Everything else that
normally protects a cycle still runs - caps, gates, the review battery,
the earx read-only guarantee, harvest-back at integration.
