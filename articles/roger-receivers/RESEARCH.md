## 2. Numeric specs (manufacturer primaries, extraction caveat applies)

NeckLoop, from the Drive (02/03) datasheet, CONFIRMED:
- Dimensions 70 x 39 x 18 mm without loop; standard loop 750 mm, short loop 550 mm.
- Weight 34 g; 52 g including standard loop.
- Battery: Lithium Polymer 3.7 VDC embedded non-removable, 250 mAh typ; charging
  time under 3 hours typ; operating time over 10 hours; charging input 5 VDC USB
  Type C, max 260 mA. User guide adds: 80 percent in 1 h, 100 percent in 3 h,
  "used for 10 hours or more" (guide wording).
- Audio bandwidth 100 Hz - 7.0 kHz; distortion under 2 percent; transmission delay
  under 20 ms; 2.4 GHz adaptive frequency hopping; RF output 2 mW; adaptive gain up
  to 20 dB; magnetic field strength 1.25 A/m at 150 mm; headphone out 3.5 mm jack,
  78 dB SPL with Phonak headphones; operating 0 to +40 C, humidity under 80 percent.
- Datasheet footnote: Check (read/manage device data via Roger Touchscreen Mic) and
  Roger Pass-around compat are "Only valid for Roger NeckLoop (02) version" - the
  one documented functional 02-vs-03 delta; everything else on the sheet is shared.
Roger X, from the Drive "Technical Data Roger X (02)" datasheet, CONFIRMED:
- 9 x 9 x 9.65 mm; 0.97 g; colour champagne (identity note only, never the
  identification method); 3-pin europlug connector.
- Powered by the hearing instrument battery, 1.0-1.5 V; current drain 2.7 mA active,
  0.5 mA sleep (VBAT 1.2 V) - i.e. it draws the aid's battery, a real-world cost.
- Audio bandwidth 100 Hz - 7.3 kHz; adaptive gain up to 20 dB; EasyGain -8 to +8 dB;
  distortion under 2 percent; backlink RF 0.02 mW; 2.4 GHz adaptive hopping.
- Edition caution: this sheet is the (02). No (03)-specific datasheet found; T26
  (repo, CONFIRMED from Rogerpedia V8 p.26) gives the delta: (03) has no EasyGain,
  no Check, no link quality measurement, narrower mic list; (02) is the fuller SKU.
  Never write "03 is the newer tier". V9 AU drops the 02/03 split editorially.
Design-integrated, current set per Rogerpedia V9 AU (repo, byte-level): Roger 14,
17, 20, 21; infant tamperproof lock integrated in Roger 20 and 21; protection
sleeve available for Roger 14. Legacy sizes (Roger 10-19) in the Drive portfolio
doc, e.g. Roger 17-era units around 0.9-1.1 g; IP67 for 19/18/15/11/10 as defined
there. CI table (V9): Naida CI M takes RogerDirect and NeckLoop; Naida CI Q takes
Roger 17, Roger X (with ComPilot, footnote 4) and NeckLoop - CI audience material.

## 3. The serial-number cutoff (GAP3 SDF3 hunt - now multiply primary)

Claim: only Roger X units above a serial boundary can carry a RogerDirect install.
- The Drive Roger X (02) datasheet states it directly: "Able to install* Roger
  receiver directly into Phonak hearing aid with RogerDirect via the Roger
  Installer ... *only available for Roger X featuring a serial number higher than
  1744XXXX". CONFIRMED (extraction caveat).
- Independently byte-level in the repo: Rogerpedia V7.00 and V8.00 texts, "via a
  Roger X (with serial number higher than 1744xxxx) and the Roger Installer"
  (V8 line 719). V9.00 AU RETAINS the Roger X + Roger Installer path (line 607)
  but DROPS the serial sentence - record the hedge: the cutoff is stated in
  2023-era primaries and the undated datasheet, not restated in the 2024 AU edition.
- Counter-claim search (deliberately hunting a different boundary or an "any Roger
  X works" claim): none found. Web corroboration instead: hearingaidknow.com
  ("first four digits 1744 or higher... champagne color") and the phonakpro FAQ /
  datasheet mirrors (snippet tier). SDF3's Phonak receivers page cite stands via
  GAP3 (HIGH). Unsettled, do not print: whether 1744 encodes year/week of
  manufacture - plausible-looking, published nowhere reached.

## 4. Licence mechanics (install, uninstall, what a second-hand buyer can verify)

From primaries and the repo's byte-verified dossiers (GAP3 is the spine):
- Two install routes: (a) microphones with unlimited (On 3, Select 3, Table Mic 3,
  Touchscreen Mic 3) or iN receivers install directly into RogerDirect-capable aids;
  (b) Roger X + Roger Installer pad (V9 line 607). Roger Installer users are
  enumerated by Phonak as HCP, School Audiologist, School Personnel - not consumers
  (GAP3 sec 1, HIGH). Route (b) is effectively HCP-gated.
- Transfer empties the source: "Once a Roger receiver's license is transferred to
  one hearing device, the Roger X is empty" (Phonak KB via GAP3). Licences move
  both ways: Rogerpedia V8 demo workflow, "Uninstall Roger from both hearing
  devices and re-install into Roger X / Roger On iN trial" (byte-level, line 606).
- Roger Unlimited (current-gen mics) removes the scarcity: unlimited receivers
  built into the transmitter, "no need to uninstall receivers ... or worry about
  re-ordering another receiver when a hearing aid is lost or replaced" (V9,
  byte-level). This is the strongest consumer-relief fact for the licence anxiety.
- Checking state, what Phonak publishes: the Installer checks full/empty (HCP list
  above); the Roger X (02) datasheet offers "Device data available via Roger
  compatible microphone with Check function" - but per T26 the Check feature exists
  on Option (02) only, and NeckLoop data reads via Touchscreen Mic only for the
  (02) version. Roger Upgrader (free Phonak software) reads device data - firmware
  version and serial number - over USB for mics/NeckLoop, but Roger X has no USB.
  Net for a second-hand buyer: no Phonak-published consumer route to verify a used
  Roger X's licence state before purchase; a used (03) cannot even be Checked by a
  mic. "Ask the seller for an HCP check, or buy where returns are possible" is the
  honest frame; tier the absence itself as verified-absent-in-reached-sources.
- UNSETTLED (two descriptions, not reconciled): the shipped Roger X 02 listing
  (operator-reviewed, "Per Phonak") says Installer full = green light, empty = no
  light; a SERP synthesis said red LED = empty slots. Primary Installer guide
  pinned in ABSENT; do not print LED semantics beyond green = licence present.
- Warranty angle for second-hand copy (shipped-listing substrate, catalogue tier):
  Roger devices carry one year as standard, two years for serialised Roger devices.
