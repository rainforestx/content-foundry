## 2. How-to-change, per mechanism (manufacturer wording)

Stick/pin type, ProWax miniFit (standard speaker, dome or Grip Tip) - Coral IFU
verbatim, read this session: "1. Tool Remove the tool from the shell. The tool has
two pins, one empty for removal and one with the new wax filter. 2. Remove Push the
empty pin into the wax filter in the speaker and pull it out. 3. Insert Insert the
new wax filter using the other pin, remove the tool and throw it out." Footnote:
"If you use a mold or LiteTip, a hearing care professional must replace the wax
filter in the speaker." Identity line: "The speaker has a white wax filter attached
to the end where the earpiece is attached."
Stick/pin type, ProWax (customized earpieces, filter sits in the mould): same
two-pin tool procedure, stated separately in the IFU; "VarioTherm Micro Mold and
LiteTip do not have a wax filter."
Rotating-disk type, CeruShield Disk (Phonak/Unitron SDS 4.0): phonak.com knowledge
base article "How to change the wax filter with CeruShield Disk" (SERP-extract of
manufacturer page, bytes not fetched - snippet-plus tier): pull earpiece off
speaker; rotate disk in arrow direction until a free opening appears under the bin
icon; insert speaker into that opening until it clicks (used protector stays in
disk); insert speaker into the opening showing a new protector, opposite the bin
icon, until it clicks. Corroborated first-party for Unitron by repo T55: Moxi V
user guide (Sonova imprint 029-6724-02/V2.0/2023-11) "Gently rotate the CeruShield
[Disk]"; CeruStop occurs zero times in that guide.
Stick type, CeruStop (Phonak/Unitron SDS 5.0/6.0): SDS 6.0 guide section heading
per T73: "Exchanging the earwax protection - CeruStop (applicable for FitnGo
receivers and custom earpieces)"; Fit and Go box parts list carries "CeruStop
098-0282". Two-ended applicator stick model (remove with empty end, insert with
loaded end) is the same family-wide pattern the repo matrix confirms across Oticon,
Signia, Beltone, Audibel sources.
CROSS-MECHANISM WARNING for the article: disk and stick systems are not
interchangeable and their tools are not proven cross-brand interchangeable even
where the cartridge is shared (matrix sec 3, HearingTracker forum corroboration).

## 3. Replacement intervals - two philosophies, both manufacturer-stated

Calendar-based (Phonak, SDS 6.0 Tips and Tricks guide via T73, first-party):
wax protection "should be replaced every four weeks on a regular receiver"; domes
"changed every three months or when it becomes stiff or brittle"; receiver exchange
"recommended... annually" (HCP judged). Zero shipped listings state these (T73).
Condition-based (Bernafon/Demant, Coral IFU read this session): NO calendar
interval for the wax filter anywhere in the booklet; trigger is "If the filter
becomes clogged, please replace" and troubleshooting rows ("Replace wax filter,
dome, or Grip Tip" for no/reduced sound). The IFU's only calendar interval is the
Grip Tip: "needs to be replaced at least once a month." The contrast is a genuine,
sourced article angle: one maker schedules, the other says replace-when-clogged.
Web snippet tier, not primary: ProWax miniFit "every 3-4 weeks" (retailer SERP
synthesis); ReSound "every 4-8 weeks or when sound weakens" (retailer/forum SERP).
Do not print these as manufacturer claims; the honest frame is "manufacturers'
own guidance ranges from a four-week schedule to replace-when-clogged".
Warranty adjacency (Coral IFU): the 12-month warranty explicitly EXCLUDES
"accessories such as batteries, tubing, speakers, earpieces and filters".

## 4. Cross-brand mechanism map (corroborates and extends the substrate census)

Spine: repo research/WAX_FILTER_CROSS_COMPAT_MATRIX.md (11x11 grid, tiered), plus
closures T74, T55, T73, AS_G8, P0_5, P0_6. Article-ready pillars:
- Demant: Oticon and Bernafon share ProWax miniFit (T74 closed this at primary
  tier via the Bernafon-named Coral IFU). Two-dimensional rule, both axes primary:
  standard speaker + dome/Grip Tip = ProWax miniFit (user-replaceable); custom
  mould/LiteTip = ProWax (HCP replaces); VarioTherm = no filter. By power: miniFit
  for 60/85 receivers, ProWax for 100/105. Article numbers: ProWax 123459, ProWax
  miniFit 130091 (miniFit Detect guide, T74). Distributor-tier 128057 also
  circulates for a ProWax miniFit "Wax Protection Set" (matrix + Drive asset
  filename) - UNRECONCILED with 130091; likely set-vs-refill numbering; do not
  print either as the sole PN without resolving. Legacy Oticon: NoWax; T-Cap and
  O-Cap for ITE styles (Drive official imagery).
- Sonova: Phonak/Unitron (and HANSATON, manufacturer-doc confirmed in matrix) share
  CeruStop 098-0282 (SDS 5.0/6.0) and CeruShield Disk (SDS 4.0; PN 098-0445 at
  snippet tier, Japebo). Receiver generation, not platform, decides - CLAUDE.md
  guardrail, first-party support both halves (T73 for 6.0, T55 for 4.0).
- WSA: Signia and Rexton share NanoCare 3.0 (PN 10993607, CONFIRMED); Audio Service
  G-series takes the same product as Connexx-branded 10993649 (AS_G8, manufacturer
  catalogue); legacy HF3/HF4 are distinct from each other and not interchangeable;
  HF4 does NOT fit Insio AX customs (P0_5 CONFIRMED-NOT-FIT). Widex NanoCare is
  name-coincidence only, different socket geometry - never claim Widex-Signia fit.
- GN: ReSound/Danalogic/Beltone/Jabra Enhance share GN WAXGUARD PN 20439700;
  Danalogic has zero own SKU (matrix, CONFIRMED).
- Starkey: HearClear, family-exclusive (Starkey/Audibel/NuEar/MicroTech); "not
  compatible with other manufacturer's devices" (matrix, manufacturer-adjacent).
- NAMING COLLISION, article-critical: at least three physically distinct products
  trade as "CeruStop" (Sonova 098-0282; GN 20439700 relabelled by resellers; Widex
  CeruSTOP XL 2.6 mm). Never use the bare name as a compatibility signal.
