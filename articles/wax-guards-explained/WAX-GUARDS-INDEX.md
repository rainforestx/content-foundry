## 1. Doctrine - this IS the subject

- Mechanism accuracy law (CLAUDE.md): cross-mechanism confusion is the #1
  catalogue defect class. SnapFit 1.0 is not 2.0, CeruShield is not CeruStop,
  ProWax is not NoWax. Adjacent mechanisms are never blurred.
- CONV sec.44 SHAPE-not-COLOUR (from LEARNING #128, revised per #145):
  identify filters by shape, never colour. Rationale in full: colour is
  unreliable two ways - region/seller variation (CeruStop ships Grey,
  Transparent, White packs) and intentional colour-match to the buyer's
  faceplate (ProWax miniFit, NoWax, T-Cap, O-Cap sell in Black/Brown/Tan/
  White to conceal against the device - colour encodes aesthetics, not
  identity). Even where colour IS stable (CeruShield disc reliably white),
  shape is used; colour adds no information. Shape lexicon (sec.44 table):
  CeruStop "small stick tool / push-in stick"; CeruShield "rotating disc
  with eight filter positions"; ProWax miniFit "integrated change tab";
  ProWax (older) "small disc on a stick tool"; NoWax "small disc on
  dispenser"; T-Cap "small cap on stick tool"; O-Cap "larger cap".
- sec.44 SCOPE is load-bearing (#145 added it): wax guards only. Functional
  colour survives - receivers' red = right / blue = left, and the HF-system
  red/blue dispenser coding (ear-side bookkeeping; the shipped generic FAQ
  warns "both HF3 and HF4 use red / blue coding, so colour alone is not the
  compatibility check"). Never generalise the ban without per-category audit.
- Receiver-generation rule (CLAUDE.md "Wax filters"): filter type follows
  the RECEIVER generation, not the platform. SDS 4.0 = CeruShield disc;
  SDS 5.0/6.0 = CeruStop stick; the same aid may carry any (set by the
  audiologist at fitting). NEVER platform-cutoff framing. Older 2-pin
  xReceiver aids (Audeo B/V/Q, Moxi Fit/Now/Kiss) use CeruStop.
- The practical tell (CONV sec.22, verbatim-adjacent): "The wax filter is
  the easiest way to tell SDS 4.0 from SDS 5.0 apart: round disc = SDS 4.0,
  small stick = SDS 5.0." Same connector, fully interchangeable. xReceiver
  (2 pins) is NOT: its CeruStop guards remain available, its wires do not.
- Shipped operationalisation (CeruStop FAQ): Marvel/Paradise/Lumity =
  "Either, depending on what receiver was fitted at dispense or repair...
  The reliable check is what you currently use"; Infinio = CeruStop (SDS
  6.0); Sonova introduced SDS 5.0 because the disc was found fiddly.
- Wax-scoped conventions born here: sec.48 tier numbers only for CURRENT
  platforms in wax picker bodies; sec.49 2x2 glance grid; sec.45
  restyle-not-remove. "Wax filter" vs "wax guard" standardisation (4,140+
  instances, LEARNING #121) still pending - shipped copy uses both.
